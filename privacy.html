FavFI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

FavFI is a personal finance planner. This page says plainly what it does with your information.

The short version

Everything you enter stays on your device. FavFI has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer cannot see your data.

What FavFI stores

The bills, cards, savings, assets and settings you type in are stored only on your iPhone or iPad, in the app's private storage. A second safety copy is kept in the app's private storage on the same device so your data survives if the app's web storage is ever cleared. Both are deleted when you use "Erase all data" in Preferences, or delete the app.

Notifications

If you turn on bill reminders, FavFI schedules local notifications on your device. They contain bill names only, never amounts, and are not sent through any server.

Face ID, Touch ID and passcode

If you turn on the privacy lock, FavFI asks iOS to confirm it is you. iOS handles the check; FavFI never receives your biometric data.

Apple Intelligence (optional)

"Ask FavFI" lets you ask questions about your numbers. By default it uses Apple's on-device model, so nothing leaves your device. If you choose Apple's cloud model and agree when asked, your question and a short summary of the numbers you see in FavFI are sent to Apple's Private Cloud Compute to produce the answer. Apple states that this data is not stored or used to train models. FavFI uses no other AI provider and sends nothing to the developer. You can use FavFI fully without ever using this feature. Apple's terms and privacy information apply to that processing.

Sharing and backups

When you export a backup or share a file, it goes through the iOS share sheet to wherever you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive. FavFI does not upload it anywhere itself.

Links

The privacy policy and help pages open in a Safari sheet. Those pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs under GitHub's own policy.

Not financial advice

FavFI gives estimates to help you plan. It is not tax, legal or investment advice.

Children

FavFI is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above will change. Questions: open an issue at github.com/favourian/favfi/issues.
