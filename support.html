Questions, bugs and ideas: open an issue on GitHub. Please don't include real account numbers or personal financial details.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your device. There is no account and no server.

How do I back up or move my data? Preferences, then Backup, then Save to Files. To restore on another device, use the import option in the same place.

How do I delete everything? Preferences, then About & legal, then Erase all data (tap twice). Deleting the app also removes its data.

Bill reminders aren't showing. Turn them on in Preferences, and allow notifications for FavFI in the iOS Settings app.

Ask FavFI says it isn't available. It needs a device with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings. The cloud option also needs iOS 27 and an internet connection.

I want to look around first. On a fresh install, Preferences, About & legal, then Load under "Try sample data".

FavFI gives estimates, not tax, legal or investment advice.
